Visitors, leads and tracking

  • Assignment: by visitor ID cookie. A visitor keeps the same variant for the whole test, including return visits.
  • After a lead form submit: the visitor is also tied to their lead ID, so the same person on another device sees the same variant once they log in or submit again.
  • Outside the rollout %, cookies blocked, or bots: see the default and aren't counted.
  • CMS admins and preview links: never counted. Preview links show a chosen variant without assigning.
  • Primary metric: counted once per visitor, credited to the variant they saw at the moment of conversion.
  • Leads: carry exp_key and variant, so leads keep their tag after the test ends or is deleted.
  • SEO: all variants keep the default's canonical tag and aren't indexed separately.
  • When a test stops: stickiness ends, and everyone sees the page default chosen in the stop dialog.
  • Blocks (Common Header, Banner Video) are shared: editing one changes every variant, even during a test.
  • One running or scheduled experiment per page. Two at once would show conflicting variants to the same visitor.
